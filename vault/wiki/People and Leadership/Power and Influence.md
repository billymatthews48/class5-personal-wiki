# Power and Influence

Billy's coursework on Power and Influence, presented in an MBA essay, analyzes the power dynamics within Bain & Co. and outlines a personal development plan for building influence. The work examines how both formal authority and informal sources like expertise and social capital contribute to power in a consulting environment.
