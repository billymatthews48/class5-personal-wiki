# Leading People

In the MBA / Leading people course, Billy engaged in teaching simulations, first covering Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) and later focusing on negotiations. Billy defined DEI as encompassing diversity of ideas, equity for marginalized groups, and inclusion, while also explaining the mechanics of negotiation, such as the reservation price and the zone of possible agreement (ZOPA).

## Key ideas
- Diversity involves having a broad mix of ideas and personal characteristics within organizations. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 1.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 1]])
- Equity in organizations means actively pursuing fair outcomes for historically marginalized communities. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 1.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 1]])
- Inclusion is about creating an environment where diverse ideas and characteristics are welcomed and utilized. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 1.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 1]])
- Lack of DEI can cause firms to lose talent and struggle to compete due to limited diversity of thought. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 1.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 1]])
- Negotiation is the process where multiple parties discuss how to distribute scarce resources. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 2.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 2]])
- The reservation price is the worst outcome a party is willing to accept during a negotiation. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 2.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 2]])
- The zone of possible agreement (ZOPA) is the overlap between parties' reservation prices, enabling a deal. ([[raw/mba/Leading people teach the AI 2.docx|MBA / Leading people teach the AI 2]])
