# Business Communication

Billy's coursework in Business Communication explored complex themes such as the tension between individual action and structural change, and the necessity of intellectual honesty when discussing issues like equity. Billy produced assignments that analyzed leadership approaches to diversity, such as Rivkin's methods, and considered the impact of cross-cultural communication challenges on student cohorts.

## Key ideas
- Effective allyship requires judgment, not just courage, necessitating thoughtful choices about when and how to intervene. ([[raw/mba/BizComm/BizCom final assignment.docx|BizComm / BizCom final assignment]])

- Weighting hiring criteria toward potential, rather than current high performers, can help attract diverse talent. ([[raw/mba/BizComm/HW2 Billy Matthews.docx|BizComm / HW2 Billy Matthews]])
