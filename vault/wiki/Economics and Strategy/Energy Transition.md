# Energy Transition

Billy produced an Energy Transition Memo that analyzes the shift in the energy landscape, focusing on the increasing role of electricity. The memo proposes an AI-assisted energy procurement platform specifically designed to assist mid-sized firms navigating these complex electrification decisions.

## Key ideas
- Electricity is now the largest supplier of useful energy, with its share rising from about 27% in 2000 to 33% in 2020. ([[raw/added/Energy Transition Memo.docx|Added / Energy Transition Memo]])
- The energy transition is driven by electrification, where sectors like transport and heating move onto the grid rather than simple fuel substitution. ([[raw/added/Energy Transition Memo.docx|Added / Energy Transition Memo]])
- Oil, gas, and coal still account for roughly 60% of useful energy as of 2020, indicating the transition is ongoing, not complete. ([[raw/added/Energy Transition Memo.docx|Added / Energy Transition Memo]])
- Electricity's lead in useful energy is partly due to efficiency, as electric end-uses waste less energy than combustion processes. ([[raw/added/Energy Transition Memo.docx|Added / Energy Transition Memo]])
- Mid-sized firms are identified as being least equipped to handle the complexities of making energy procurement decisions. ([[raw/added/Energy Transition Memo.docx|Added / Energy Transition Memo]])
