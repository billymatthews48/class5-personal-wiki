# Brand Management

Billy explored brand management through two main lenses: developing a lifestyle brand strategy for Avocados from Mexico and defining the brand essence for Quest Nutrition. For Avocados from Mexico, the goal was to shift perception from a commodity to a lifestyle brand, while for Quest, the focus was on positioning the brand around 'indulgence without Compromise.'

## Key ideas
- Avocados from Mexico needs to transition from being seen as a functional commodity to an emotionally connected lifestyle brand. ([[raw/mba/Marketing/Hwk 3.docx|Marketing / Hwk 3]])
- The target audience for Avocados from Mexico are health-conscious, lifestyle-forward consumers aged 25–45. ([[raw/mba/Marketing/Hwk 3.docx|Marketing / Hwk 3]])
- Quest Nutrition aims to own the 'good cheating' position in healthy snacking by positioning itself as 'Indulgence without Compromise.' ([[raw/mba/Marketing/OGSM Billy Matthews.docx|Marketing / OGSM Billy Matthews]])
- Quest's 'Flip the macros' philosophy transforms unhealthy foods into high-protein, low-carb, and low-sugar versions. ([[raw/mba/Marketing/SBM HW 2.pdf|Marketing / SBM HW 2]])
- A personal brand statement emphasizes authenticity paired with continuous improvement as a core essence. ([[raw/mba/Marketing/Personal Project.docx|Marketing / Personal Project]])
- The strategy for Quest involves launching an integrated campaign called 'It’s Basically Cheating' across multiple channels. ([[raw/mba/Marketing/OGSM Billy Matthews.pdf|Marketing / OGSM Billy Matthews]])
